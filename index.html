Cosmos: Fundamental Physical Structures

The Cosmos pillar focuses on synthesizing physical reality from fundamental principles. It incorporates computational cosmology, theoretical physics models (including the Wolfram Physics Project framework), quantum gravity architectures, and the overarching mathematical structures governing our universe.

Key focus areas include non-contradictory scientific models, space-time representations, and deep physical constants.

Nowness: Present State & Human Identity

The Nowness pillar captures the contemporary state of human knowledge, consciousness, and institutional identity. It acts as an active snapshot of current scientific consensus, human expression, civic initiatives, and observational paradigms.

It grounds abstract cosmological principles in real-time civilizational progress, ethics, and knowledge synthesis.

Eternities: Deep-Time Preservation

The Eternities pillar addresses the challenge of deep-time information archiving. It designs data structures, digital/physical preservation mechanisms, and decentralized record hosts capable of surviving institutional decay and civilizational shifts.

Official documentation, dataset registries, and public archives are linked across persistent repositories, including Zenodo and decentralized archives.
