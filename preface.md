## 1. Context & The 400-Year Epistemic Lag

Since approximately 1600 A.D., humanity has experienced an explosive, exponential expansion in empirical scientific capability and technological power. However, our social, political, and spiritual institutions have lagged behind, creating a 400-year epistemic gap. 

When technological capability is severed from moral philosophy and deep-time awareness, existential risks arise. To heal this rift, we must re-anchor human discovery within a framework that harmonizes physical cosmology with an unshakeable ethical baseline: **"Compassion is the crown of wisdom."**

## 2. The 12 Foundational Axioms

1. **The Relational Canvas:** Space and motion are primary; time ($t$) is an internal biological bookkeeping mechanism rather than a fundamental physical container.
2. **The Invariant Metronome ($\Gamma_0$):** Deep-time calibration relies on physical constants—specifically the spin-flip hyperfine transition of neutral atomic hydrogen ($L_0 \approx 21.11 \text{ cm}, \tau_0 \approx 0.704 \text{ ns}$).
3. **Subsidiary Books of Religion:** Natural Philosophy (Science and Mathematics) represents *Changing Knowledge*—a dynamic, self-correcting subsidiary guide to the physical canvas, leaving primary sacred scriptures to hold unmoving moral and spiritual baselines.
4. **Universal Accessibility:** Human scientific knowledge belongs to no single corporation, nation, or species; it is a public-domain trust for all intelligent life.
5. **The Non-Ownership Principle:** No individual or institution owns the cosmic framework; authority rests in open-source, decentralized verification.
6. **Abolition of Cruelty:** Technological or scientific progress that causes deliberate suffering to biological life is a violation of cosmic order.
7. **The Relational Universe:** All physical coordinates are interconnected; local actions exert structural influence across the spatial canvas.
8. **Decentralized Archiving:** Critical civilizational records must be distributed across persistent public nodes (such as Zenodo and distributed digital networks) to withstand institutional decay.
9. **Complementarity of Faith and Reason:** Empirical observation of creation enhances spiritual reverence rather than diminishing it.
10. **The Neutral Handshake:** Communication across deep time must utilize non-arbitrary physical constants rather than ephemeral human languages or calendars.
11. **Evolving Consensus:** Scientific models must remain open to continuous refinement as observational resolution improves.
12. **The Ethical Imperative:** Power without compassion is self-destructive; moral stewardship must guide all technological deployment.
